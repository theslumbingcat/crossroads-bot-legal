# 🌟 Crossroads Bot ✦

**One community. Many possibilities.**

Crossroads Bot is an all-in-one Discord bot built for **The Crossroads** — a friendly, SFW community where people can chat, make friends, play games, and spend time together.

From server moderation and member verification to private support tickets, reminders, leveling, polls, and mini-games, Crossroads Bot brings useful community tools together in one place.

Our goal is simple: **make managing a community easier and make being part of it more fun.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛡️ Moderation & Safety
Tools to help moderators manage the server, maintain order, and keep the community welcoming and safe.

### ✅ Member Verification
Support for member verification and onboarding, helping new members get started with the community.

### 🎫 Private Tickets
A ticket system for support requests, questions, and discreet reports of problems.

### ⏰ Reminders & Utilities
Useful commands and interactive tools to make everyday server activities easier.

### 📊 Leveling & Community Activity
Leveling features that encourage participation and give active members a way to track their progress.

### 📊 Polls & Community Tools
Interactive features that help members share opinions, participate in discussions, and engage with the community.

### 🎮 Mini-Games & Entertainment
Take a break from chatting with a collection of games and interactive challenges, including:

- 🪙 Coin flips and dice games
- ✊ Rock Paper Scissors
- 🧠 Trivia, riddles, and math quizzes
- 🔢 Number guessing and word games
- 🃏 Blackjack and higher-or-lower
- 🎭 Truth or Dare and Would You Rather
- ⚡ Reaction tests and memory challenges
- ❌⭕ Tic-Tac-Toe

And more fun ways to interact with other members!

## 💫 Why Crossroads Bot?

- **All-in-one:** Community tools and entertainment in a single bot.
- **Easy to use:** Discord slash commands and interactive features.
- **Community-focused:** Built around the needs of The Crossroads.
- **Safety-conscious:** Includes tools for moderation, verification, and private support.
- **Made for fun:** Encourages members to participate and connect.
