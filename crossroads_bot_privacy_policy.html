Privacy Policy

For Crossroads Bot · Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Crossroads Bot may process when it is used in a Discord server, why that information is used, and what choices you have.

1. About This Policy

Crossroads Bot is a community bot intended to support server features such as moderation, verification, tickets and reports, reminders, leveling, and games. The features enabled in a particular server determine which information the bot processes.

In this policy, “we” means the person or team operating Crossroads Bot. Discord is a separate service and is not controlled by the bot operator.

2. Information the Bot May Process

Depending on the features enabled and how the bot is configured, it may process:

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID and the IDs of the server, channel, role, or message needed to carry out a feature.
  • Server settings: configuration choices such as selected channels, verification roles, ticket settings, and reminder settings.
  • Moderation records: warnings or other moderation actions, the relevant user IDs, reasons entered by moderators, and timestamps, when moderation logging is enabled.
  • Tickets and reports: the messages and details you submit through a ticket or report, along with information needed to route and respond to it.
  • Feature data: leveling or experience totals, game results, and other progress or settings if those features are enabled and store such information.
  • Command information: the information you submit to a slash command, such as a reminder time or game choice, to provide the requested response.

The bot may receive message events when a feature needs them, for example to award experience points. It is not intended to keep a general archive of ordinary conversations. The actual information processed depends on the bot's code, permissions, and enabled features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • run commands and provide the server features you request;
  • operate verification, moderation, ticket, and reporting workflows;
  • maintain reminders, levels, game progress, and server configuration where applicable;
  • prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect the stability of the bot; and
  • respond to privacy or support requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use ticket or report contents for advertising.

4. Tickets, Reports, and Sensitive Messages

Only share information in a ticket or report that is relevant to the issue. Avoid sending passwords, payment information, government identification, or other highly sensitive details.

Ticket and report contents may be visible to the people who have permission to access the relevant channels or records. A ticket should not be assumed to be confidential from server staff who are authorised to review it.

5. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the bot's database or files on the machine or hosting service where the bot runs. It may remain stored while needed to provide a feature, preserve moderation records, maintain server settings, or resolve an issue.

Retention depends on the bot's current implementation and configuration. Some information may remain in backups or logs for a limited period after it is removed from active storage. Do not publish this policy until you have checked where your bot stores data and how your hosting provider handles backups and logs.

6. Access, Deletion, and Your Choices

You can choose not to use optional bot commands or features. You can also contact us to ask what information the bot may have stored about you or to request deletion.

We may need your Discord user ID, the server involved, and enough detail to identify the relevant records. We will review requests and delete information where reasonably possible, subject to legitimate moderation, security, technical, or legal needs. Removing the bot from a server does not necessarily delete records already stored by the bot or its hosting provider.

To make a request, email theslumbingcat@gmail.com.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Crossroads Bot uses Discord to operate within Discord servers. The bot may also rely on a hosting provider or other technical service used by its operator. Those services may process information as needed to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect users or the service, address abuse, comply with a valid legal requirement, or resolve a security incident. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

8. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to restrict access to stored bot data, protect bot credentials, and limit access to private ticket and report channels. However, no online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keep bot tokens and hosting credentials private. If you operate the bot, make sure your actual setup follows these practices.

9. Children and Mixed-Age Communities

The Crossroads community is intended to remain SFW and may include people of different ages. Users must meet Discord's applicable age requirements and follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Do not use the bot to request or share a minor's private information.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revision is made. Please review this page periodically.
